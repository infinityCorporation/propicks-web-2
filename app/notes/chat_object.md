## Chat Object

The chat object will require the following information sorted into two groups, necessary and possible.
The necessary traits will be needed on launch while the possible traits are for future capabilities.

### Necessary
1. post_id
2. username
3. forum_name
4. post_timestamp
5. post_content
6. post_user_pfp (link to hosted pfp)
7. likes_count
8. reply_count
9. attached_bet_object (if applicable)
10. user_id

### Possible
1. attached_media_link (if photo/video posting added)
2. attached_poll_id (if community polls added)
3. attached_parlay_id (if parlays addded)
4. type_pick (denote the post type as a standard post or a pick post)
